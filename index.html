Support healthy function

Daily Exercises

A gentle daily rhythm built around your conditions — not a promise, a pattern worth repeating.
Metabolic and cardiovascular health
The habits in this routine are associated with better sleep timing, insulin sensitivity, muscle maintenance, cardiovascular fitness and overall wellbeing.
Consistency over intensity
Small actions repeated consistently are the goal. The routine avoids promises such as reversing ageing, treating fatigue or directly renewing mitochondria.
Benefits supported by the routine
Energy rhythm
Morning daylight supports circadian rhythm, sleep timing and daytime alertness, which can indirectly support metabolic health.
Muscle and metabolism
Movement and strength training help maintain muscle, improve insulin sensitivity, support mitochondrial content in muscle and reduce age-related muscle loss.
Recovery and repair
Sleep supports recovery, immune function, cognition and many cellular repair processes. Slow breathing can support stress regulation.
Your Daily Exercises
Ten gentle mobility exercises, one for each area covered on the KindFare website — informed by NHS and NICE patient guidance for these areas, not a substitute for it. Stop if you feel sharp or worsening pain, and check with your GP or a physiotherapist first if you're recovering from a recent injury.
Chin Tucks & Slow Neck Turns Neck
Sit or stand tall. Gently draw your chin straight back (like making a double chin), hold 3 seconds, release — 8–10 reps. Follow with slow head turns left and right within a comfortable range, 5 each side.
Wall Slides & Pendulum Swings Shoulder
Stand facing a wall, forearms resting against it, and slide your arms slowly upward as far as comfortable, then back down — 8 reps. Follow with gentle pendulum swings: lean forward slightly, let one arm hang and swing in small circles, 10 each direction.
Wrist Extensor & Flexor Stretch Elbow
Extend one arm in front of you, palm down, and gently pull the fingers back toward you with the other hand until you feel a stretch along the top of the forearm. Hold 20–30 seconds, then turn the palm up and repeat underneath. Twice each side.
Median Nerve Glide & Wrist Circles Wrist
With your arm out and elbow straight, gently bend and straighten your wrist and fingers through a comfortable range, 8–10 times. Follow with slow wrist circles, 5 each direction, both hands.
Thumb Opposition & Grip Squeeze Hand & Fingers
Touch your thumb to the tip of each finger in turn, 2 rounds per hand. Follow with a gentle squeeze of a soft ball or rolled towel, holding 3–5 seconds, 10 squeezes per hand.
Cat-Cow & Knee-to-Chest Lower Back & Pelvis
On hands and knees, alternate gently arching and rounding your back with your breath, 8–10 slow rounds. Then lying on your back, draw one knee toward your chest, hold 15–20 seconds, and swap sides.
Supported Bridge & Pelvic Tilts SI Joint
Lying on your back with knees bent, gently tilt your pelvis to flatten your lower back into the floor, then release — 10 reps. Follow with a gentle bridge: lift your hips a small amount, hold 3–5 seconds, lower slowly. 8 reps.
Standing Hip Circles & Sit-to-Stand Hip
Holding a chair for balance, make slow, small circles with one knee lifted, 5 each direction, each leg. Then practise sit-to-stand from a firm chair, 8–10 controlled reps, using your hands as little as possible.
Seated Knee Extensions & Mini Squats Knee
Sitting in a chair, slowly straighten one knee until level, hold 3 seconds, lower — 10 reps each leg. Follow with shallow mini squats holding a stable surface, 8–10 reps, only as deep as feels comfortable.
Calf Stretch & Towel Scrunches Foot & Ankle
Stand facing a wall, one foot back with the heel down, and lean gently forward until you feel a stretch in the calf. Hold 20–30 seconds, twice each side. Then, seated, scrunch a towel toward you using just your toes, 2–3 rounds each foot.
